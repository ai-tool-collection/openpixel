Logos in this folder identify the apps OpenPixel is compared with. They come from each project's own website,
repository or store page, resized to 256×256:

- Aseprite, Pixelorama, MagicaVoxel: their official websites
- LibreSprite: github.com/LibreSprite/LibreSprite (desktop icon)
- Piskel: github.com/piskelapp/piskel (src/logo.png)
- Goxel: github.com/guillaumechereau/goxel (data/icons/icon256.png)
- Lospec Pixel Editor: lospec.com (site icon)
- Pixilart: pixilart.com (site icon)
- PixelLab: pixellab.ai (site icon)
- Pyxel Edit: pyxeledit.com (site icon)
- Pro Motion NG: cosmigo.com (the Cosmigo symbol, the maker of Pro Motion NG)

compares itself with; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
